# World Bible

## The setting

The dawn of tools. One small tribe, one cave, one fire, one river. The
world is small on purpose — a handful of tiles, a handful of people. The
drama is close: a cough, a missed hunt, a stick that finally finds the
root.

sick — the clock on their knowledge is already running.

## The "cave tongue" style guide

The voice is the game's signature. Rules:

- **Short. Present tense. Physical.** "FIRE LIVE." not "The fire is burning now."
- **Subject-verb, drop the articles.** "ROOT HIDE LOW." "STONE BITE WOOD."
- **All caps for spoken/emphatic lines.** Lowercase is fine for helper UI text.
- **Emoji as nouns.** 🔥 is fire, 🪏 is the digging tool, 🐾 is tracks/meat-near. Consistent icon = consistent meaning.
- **One idea per line.** If it needs a comma-and, it's two lines.
- **Meaning over tally.** The number row already said "MEAT +2." The voice says what it *cost* or what it *meant*: "MEAT FALL. HOME."
- **Loss is quiet.** Don't melodramatize death. "YOUNG SIT BY 🪏. NO WORD." The restraint is the weight.

## Weather & time as pressure

Weather isn't flavor, it's a scheduled squeeze. TEACH's 10-sun arc bakes in
a rhythm the unified game should keep as its "seasonal" texture:

- Sun 3: 🌧️ rain — mud slows digging, fire drops, old cough worsens.
- Sun 5–8: 🐾 tracks — meat is near, hunts pay off.
- Sun 6: ❄️ cold — warmth tool suddenly matters.
- Sun 7: 💧 water low — river walk needed.
- Sun 9: old bones heavy — last real window to teach before loss.

## Tone north star

Warm, not grim. It's about a family keeping each other alive and passing
down what they know. Hardship is real and permanent, but the frame is
tenderness: MA sees cold before cold; the young want to make the old proud.
